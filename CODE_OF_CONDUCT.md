# Code of conduct

CyberStage is a collaborative project for developers, designers, writers, educators, filmmakers, and security practitioners. We want participation to be technically rigorous and personally respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- State safety, accessibility, and correctness concerns directly and with evidence.
- Welcome contributors with different levels of technical or security experience.
- Give credit for creative, design, documentation, and testing work.
- Keep examples fictional and avoid exposing private or operational security information.
- Respect a contributor's boundaries and preferred form of address.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate humiliation, doxxing, release of private information, and sustained disruption are not accepted. Neither is using project spaces to request live-target intrusion assistance or to distribute actionable malicious material.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community or the project's safety boundary.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's available GitHub contact channel. Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive details.

Maintainers should handle reports proportionately, preserve privacy where possible, and avoid conflicts of interest.
